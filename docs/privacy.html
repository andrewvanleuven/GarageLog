GarageLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

GarageLog is a personal vehicle maintenance tracking app. This policy explains what happens to your data.

No account, no data collection

GarageLog does not require you to create an account or sign in. The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any of your data. There is no analytics, tracking, or advertising SDK in the app.

Where your data lives

All information you enter — vehicles, maintenance logs, fuel fill-ups, reminders, receipt photos, and vehicle photos — is stored locally on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData framework. Nothing is transmitted to the developer or to any third-party server.

iCloud sync (planned)

A future version of GarageLog will optionally sync your data across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit private database. When enabled, your data will be stored in the private iCloud storage tied to your own Apple ID — encrypted in transit and at rest — and will only be accessible to you, signed in with your Apple ID. The developer will not have access to this data at any point. This feature will be optional and off by default.

Data export

GarageLog lets you export your own data (as CSV or PDF) directly from the app for your own backup or records. These exports are created and saved locally on your device, or shared via the standard iOS share sheet to a destination you choose — the developer is not involved in or aware of this process.

Third parties

GarageLog does not share, sell, or transmit any of your data to third parties, because it does not transmit your data anywhere at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, once iCloud sync ships — this page will be updated to reflect it.
